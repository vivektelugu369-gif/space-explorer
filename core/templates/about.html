Beyond the Stars

Welcome to Space Explorer — a journey through the universe powered by technology and curiosity.

Mission Briefing

Space Explorer is a web application that allows users to discover the wonders of space through NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day API.

Users can explore astronomical pictures by date, view explanations, create favourites and manage their personal accounts.
